Depending on the commands and features you use, the bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, command inputs, and information needed to respond to commands.

Information may be processed to run commands, provide minigames, support moderation and management features, and troubleshoot problems.

3. Storage and Retention

Information may be handled by Discord and the bot hosting service. How long information is retained depends on the bot's actual configuration and the services used.

4. Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the bot or comply with applicable law.

5. Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect bot credentials and any stored information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

Users must follow Discord's age requirements and applicable laws when using the bot.

7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as IbrahimYT's features or information-handling practices change.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the bot owner through the official support channel, when available.
